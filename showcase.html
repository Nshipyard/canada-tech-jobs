Seven questions, seven charts

The core findings of the State of the tech job market in Canada (Late 2026), one question at a time. Each card states the answer in one line and shows the chart behind it.

Takeaway 1

Are tech vacancies rising in Canada?

Yes, narrowly. Total vacancies fell from 1,032,215 in Q2 2022 to 548,400 in Q2 2026, down 46.9%, in the direct StatCan WDS series. Natural and applied sciences vacancies reached 43,400 in Q2 2026, up 5.7% from Q1 and up 6.0% year over year. Software developers stood at 3,545, up 925 from a year earlier. Canada has no PM openings series, so no Canada PM win can be claimed.

Source: Statistics Canada, table 14-10-0444-01, WDS direct pull Nov 11, 2026. Unadjusted, quarterly, Q1 2022 to Q2 2026.
Takeaway 2

Is software hiring weak?

Yes. Indeed Canada software development postings fell 8.0% year to date to Sep 30, 2026, and sit 22% below the pre-pandemic baseline, at an index of 78. Software engineers fell from 9,480 in Q2 2022 to 2,625 in Q2 2026, down 72.3%. Engineering postings, by contrast, are rising.

Source: Indeed Hiring Lab Canada, Q3 2026 update.
Takeaway 3

How is design doing in Canada?

Graphic designers fell hard. Vacancies fell from 2,995 in Q2 2022 to 770 in Q2 2026, down 74.3%. Lenny counts about 1,900 open product design roles worldwide, down 28% in a year. Canada still has no product design count. Job Bank rates the software outlook Very limited in Toronto and Ontario, and Good in Nova Scotia.

Source: Job Bank, outlooks 2025-2027.
Takeaway 4

Does everybody now need AI?

AI words appear in 19.0% of all Canadian Indeed postings on Sep 30, 2026, up from 5.7% a year earlier. Ontario sits at 28%, but Ontario's Jan 2026 disclosure rule drives much of the jump. Read the 19% with that caveat.

Source: Indeed Hiring Lab AI Tracker, to Sep 30, 2026.
Takeaway 5

Where does Canadian tech talent concentrate?

Toronto ranks 3rd in North America, and six Canadian cities sit in the top 15. Toronto, Montreal, and Vancouver hold 60% of Canada's AI jobs. Concentration is real, but it is spread across six cities, not one Bay Area. CBRE is a private report, shown as secondary context only.

Source: CBRE, Scoring Tech Talent 2026. Private report, secondary context only.
Takeaway 6

Is remote work gone?

Nearly. Only 4% of Canadian technology postings in Q2 2026 were fully remote. Hybrid was 16% and fully on-site was 80%. This report makes no record claim, because no longer series is shown. Robert Half via TalentNeuron is a private panel.

Source: Robert Half Canada via TalentNeuron, Q4 2025 and Q2 2026. Private panel, secondary context only.
Takeaway 7

Where is the growth, if not in software?

Outside software. Civil engineering postings rose 30% year over year. Manufacturing vacancies rose 17.1% and trades vacancies rose 6.6%. Health vacancies fell 7.8% and software postings fell 8.0% year to date.

Sources: Statistics Canada JVWS Q2 2026; Indeed Hiring Lab Canada Q3 2026.
