Total vacancies fell 46.9% from the 2022 peak

1,032,215 in Q2 2022 to 548,400 in Q2 2026. Total vacancies fell from 1,032,215 in Q2 2022 to 548,400 in Q2 2026, down 46.9%, in the direct StatCan WDS series, 18 quarters, Q1 2022 to Q2 2026. Natural and applied sciences vacancies reached 43,400 in Q2 2026, up 6.0% from a year earlier.

Source: Statistics Canada, table 14-10-0444-01, WDS direct pull Nov 11, 2026. Unadjusted, quarterly, Q1 2022 to Q2 2026.
