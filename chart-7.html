Growth sits outside software, in engineering, trades, and manufacturing

Manufacturing up 17.1% year over year, trades up 6.6%. Civil engineering postings rose 30% year over year. Industrial, electrical, and mechanical engineering postings each rose 18%. Manufacturing vacancies rose 17.1% year over year. Trades vacancies rose 6.6%. Software postings fell 8.0% year to date.

Sources: Statistics Canada JVWS Q2 2026 (WDS direct pull); Indeed Hiring Lab Canada Q3 2026.
